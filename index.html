Every visit is a signal — the pages someone lingers on, the pricing tab they reopen, the third visit this week. SalesIQ reads those signals live, then routes the moment to a human, a bot, or a report, whichever gets your visitor an answer fastest.

From anonymous tab to closed deal, in seven steps.

  3. 03

    Chat initiated

    A trigger fires at the right behavioral moment — not a random delay.

  4. 04

  5. 05
